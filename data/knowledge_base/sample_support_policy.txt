
RESOLVEAI CUSTOMER SUPPORT POLICY

1. Duplicate Payments
If a customer is charged twice for the same order, verify both transactions using the order ID, payment dates, and transaction references. Once a duplicate charge is confirmed, initiate a refund for the extra payment.

2. Refund Requests
Customers can request refunds for eligible orders. Support agents must verify the order details and payment status before approving a refund.

3. Delivery Issues
For delayed or missing deliveries, verify the order ID and delivery status. Escalate unresolved delivery issues to the operations team.

4. Account Issues
For account access problems, verify the registered email address before assisting with account recovery.

5. Customer Communication
Always respond politely, acknowledge the customer's concern, and explain the next steps clearly.
